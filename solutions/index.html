Where can the technology be applied?

Lorgani is built for the information-intensive work of regulated Life Sciences. Explore a catalogue of 50 Life Sciences use cases across 6 domains. A named application is shown where one addresses a use case today; the others are areas where the platform can be applied.

Regulatory & Safety Dossiers

Pharmacovigilance (PV), drug safety and regulatory teams produce some of the most demanding documents in Life Sciences - aggregate reports such as the DSUR, PBRER/PSUR and PADER, signal evaluations and risk management plans: recurring, deadline-driven, and assembled from evidence spread across many systems and functions. Each cycle repeats much of the last one while demanding that everything new is found, assessed and explained.

Related application: iDossi - Intelligent Dossiers

  • DSUR Development Safety Update Report - the annual review of safety information for investigational products (ICH E2F).
    iDossi
  • PBRER / PSUR Periodic Benefit-Risk Evaluation Report - periodic evaluation of the benefit-risk balance of a marketed product (ICH E2C(R2)).
    iDossi
  • PADER / PAER Periodic adverse drug experience reporting for marketed products in the United States.
    iDossi
  • Risk Management Plan Safety specification, pharmacovigilance plan and risk-minimisation measures, maintained over the product lifecycle.
    Lorgani
  • Signal Evaluation Report Documenting the evidence behind a potential safety signal and its assessment, as part of signal management.
    Lorgani
  • CTD Nonclinical Overview / Summaries Nonclinical overview and written and tabulated summaries within the Common Technical Document.
    Lorgani
  • Quality Overall Summary The summary of chemistry, manufacturing and controls information within the Common Technical Document.
    Lorgani
  • CMC Regulatory Sections Chemistry, manufacturing and controls content for submissions and variations.
    Lorgani
  • Regulatory Response / Health Authority Questions Evidence-based responses to health authority questions within committed timelines.
    Lorgani
  • Regulatory Briefing Package Briefing documents that prepare for scientific advice and health authority meetings.
    Lorgani
  • Labeling / CCDS / CCSI Assessment Assessing core company data sheets, core safety information and product labeling against emerging evidence.
    Lorgani
  • Label Change Impact Assessment Understanding which documents, markets and materials a labeling change affects.
    Lorgani
  • PASS / PAES Report Reporting for post-authorisation safety and efficacy studies.
    Lorgani
  • Benefit-Risk Assessment Structured benefit-risk documentation drawing on efficacy, safety and exposure evidence.
    Lorgani
  • Safety Narrative Generation Case narratives for serious adverse events, adverse drug reactions and events of special interest, prepared for expert review.
    Lorgani
  • Aggregate SAE / AESI Review Aggregate review of serious adverse events and adverse events of special interest across a programme.
    Lorgani
  • Submission Content Reuse / Country Dossier Adapting approved core content consistently across markets and submissions.
    Lorgani

Clinical & Medical Documentation

Clinical development generates large, interdependent document sets: protocols and their amendments, statistical outputs, study reports and the summaries built from them. Each document draws on the others, and consistency across them matters as much as the content of any one.

Related application: CliDossi - Intelligent Clinical Dossiers

  • Clinical Study Report (CSR) The integrated report of a clinical study’s methods and results.
    Lorgani
  • CSR Synopsis A concise summary of a study report for submissions and disclosure.
    Lorgani
  • Clinical Trial Protocol The study design, objectives and procedures.
    Lorgani
  • Protocol Amendment Documenting changes to a protocol and their rationale.
    Lorgani
  • Investigator’s Brochure The compilation of clinical and nonclinical data on an investigational product.
    Lorgani
  • Informed Consent / Patient Information Participant-facing documents that explain a study clearly and accurately.
    Lorgani
  • Clinical Development Plan The strategy and sequence of studies for a development programme.
    Lorgani
  • CTD Module 2 Clinical Overview The critical assessment of clinical data within the Common Technical Document.
    Lorgani
  • CTD Module 2 Clinical Summary The detailed factual summary of clinical information within the Common Technical Document.
    Lorgani
  • Data Monitoring Committee Package Documentation prepared for independent data monitoring committee review.
    Lorgani
  • Clinical Trial Disclosure Summary Results and lay summaries for public trial registries and disclosure obligations.
    Lorgani

Literature & Evidence Intelligence

Medical and scientific teams work from a constantly growing body of published literature, internal data and real-world evidence. The value is in connecting it: finding what is relevant, seeing how findings relate, and turning that into answers people can rely on.

  • Literature Intelligence / Review Ongoing identification and review of relevant scientific and medical literature.
    Lorgani
  • Medical Information Response Evidence-based answers to questions from healthcare professionals.
    Lorgani
  • Scientific / Medical Literature Review Summarizing and comparing published evidence on a defined scientific or medical question.
    Lorgani
  • Systematic / Targeted Literature Review Literature reviews with documented search, screening and synthesis methodology.
    Lorgani
  • HEOR / Value Dossier Organizing evidence on outcomes, burden of disease and value.
    Lorgani
  • HTA Submission Support Assembling evidence for health technology assessment submissions.
    Lorgani
  • Real-World Evidence Study Report Reporting the methods and findings of real-world evidence studies.
    Lorgani
  • Regulatory Intelligence Assessment Assessing new guidance and regulatory developments for their relevance and impact.
    Lorgani

Content, Data & Analytics

Much of the information Life Sciences organizations depend on lives in documents, reports and unstructured content rather than databases. Turning it into structured, comparable data - and then into analysis and reporting - is usually manual, repetitive and hard to trust.

Related application: ConDatics - Content → Data → Analytics

  • Due Diligence / Asset Evaluation Bringing together and structuring the information needed to evaluate a product, asset or opportunity.
    Lorgani
  • Technology / Product Transfer Dossier Organizing the knowledge that must move with a product or process between sites or organizations.
    Lorgani
  • Knowledge Transfer / Product Knowledge Dossier Capturing product knowledge in a structured, reusable and traceable form.
    Lorgani

Quality & Compliance

Quality and compliance teams manage a continuous flow of procedures, events, investigations and reviews. Each one produces documentation that must be accurate, consistent and ready for inspection - and each one should make the quality system stronger.

    Lorgani
  • SOP Impact Assessment Identifying procedures and records affected by a change.
    Lorgani
  • Deviation / Investigation Report Documenting events and their investigation consistently.
    Lorgani
  • CAPA Report / Effectiveness Review Corrective and preventive action documentation and effectiveness follow-up.
    Lorgani
  • Audit / Inspection Response Preparing evidence-based responses to audit and inspection findings.
    Lorgani
  • Product Quality Review / APQR Annual product quality review documentation.
    Lorgani
  • Quality Risk Assessment Structured documentation of quality risks and their controls.
    Lorgani
  • Complaint / Trend Investigation Investigating complaints and trends and documenting the conclusions.
