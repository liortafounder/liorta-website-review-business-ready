Quality & Compliance

Quality-system documentation that keeps pace with the organization.

Quality and compliance teams manage a continuous flow of procedures, events, investigations and reviews. Each one produces documentation that must be accurate, consistent and ready for inspection - and each one should make the quality system stronger.

The challenge

Where the effort goes today

  1. 01

    Procedures change constantly, and assessing the impact of each change is laborious.

  2. 02

    Deviations, investigations and CAPAs generate documentation that must be consistent and complete.

  3. 03

    Audits and inspections demand fast, well-evidenced responses.

What Liorta provides

Lorgani™, applied to Quality & Compliance.

Outcomes, not a black box: organized evidence, structured drafts, traceable content and governed review - with people making every decision that matters.

Structured quality documentation

Consistent drafts for procedures, investigations and reviews.

Impact visibility

Related procedures and records surfaced when something changes.

Evidence-ready responses

Audit and inspection responses prepared with their supporting evidence.

Quality ownership

Quality professionals review, decide and approve.
