AI & Trust

Protection designed into AI-assisted intelligence.

In regulated Life Sciences, intelligence is only useful if it can be trusted. Lorgani is built so that AI can work on complex, sensitive information while protection, traceability, governance and human accountability remain part of the experience — not an afterthought.

  • Protection — Designated sensitive and confidential information is safeguarded throughout AI-assisted work.
  • Controlled AI interaction — AI operates within governed boundaries rather than open-ended use.
  • Traceability — Outcomes stay connected to the evidence they came from.
  • Enterprise access — Access follows roles and organizational controls.
  • Human accountability — Qualified people review, decide and approve.

Liorta Protection Engine™

Designed in from the start.

The Liorta Protection Engine is designed to help safeguard designated sensitive and confidential information throughout AI-assisted work, so that intelligence can be applied without unnecessary exposure.

It is part of how the platform works rather than a setting added later — designed as a shared platform capability available across Lorgani applications.

AI designed into the platform

AI prepares. People decide.

What AI contributes

  • Understanding and organizing large volumes of source information
  • Preparing high-quality, structured drafts for expert review
  • Keeping content connected to its supporting evidence
  • Applying automated quality and consistency checks

What stays with people

  • Interpreting evidence and drawing conclusions
  • Reviewing, editing and approving every output
  • Decisions with regulatory, clinical or patient impact
  • Accountability for what is submitted or communicated

Responsible use

Powerful AI. Evidence-connected. Expert-governed.

Lorgani is designed to produce high-quality, evidence-connected outputs, with expert review embedded at critical points to strengthen confidence, traceability and governance.

AI brings speed, scale and intelligence to information-intensive work, while expert review and approval remain embedded where professional judgement and accountability matter.

How customer content and data are handled is agreed with each customer and documented as part of the engagement.

Our security and trust approach is grounded in verifiable controls, transparent governance and recognized standards, with certifications and regulatory references used where independently validated.
