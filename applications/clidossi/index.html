CliDossi in context

Many kinds of clinical evidence. One structured clinical document.

Protocols, statistical outputs, summaries and published evidence converge into a document that clinical and medical experts review and approve.

The challenge

Why clinical documentation needs a different approach

Clinical documents are built from many interdependent sources - protocols and amendments, statistical outputs, summaries and published evidence - and must stay consistent as those sources change. Most of the effort goes into bringing that evidence together before the real clinical writing can start.

What CliDossi helps you do

From clinical evidence to an approved document - with people in control.

Specific supported document types are confirmed with each customer. Talk to us about the clinical documentation you want to address.

  1. 01

    Clinical evidence brought together

    Sources relevant to a clinical document organized around it.

  2. 02

    Structured clinical drafts

    Content prepared in the expected structure as a starting point for clinical and medical experts.

  3. 03

    Consistency across documents

    Related clinical documents kept aligned as the evidence evolves.

  4. 04

    Traceability

    Statements linked back to the outputs and sources behind them.

  5. 05

    Expert review and approval

    Clinicians and medical writers remain the authors and approvers.
