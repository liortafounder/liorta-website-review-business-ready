About Liorta

Technology that keeps Life Sciences moving.

Liorta — pronounced “Li-or-ta” — begins with Li, for Life. Just as the aorta carries life through the body, we aim to be the lifeline that carries innovation, efficiency and transformation through Life Sciences.

Our origin

Built by people who have spent careers inside Life Sciences and technology.

After decades of leadership in Life Sciences, medicine, software and global service delivery, our founders saw a recurring pattern: organizations pioneering advances in healthcare and medicines were diverting precious focus to managing complex technology, compliance demands and operational inefficiency.

That sparked a question: what if Life Sciences organizations could concentrate fully on science and growth, while a trusted technology partner handled the complexity?

Technology should work for Life Sciences — not the other way around. Liorta exists to help organizations focus on what matters most: transforming lives through science.

Experience

Domain experience behind everything we build.

Liorta did not begin with technology and then search for a Life Sciences problem to solve. Our platform and applications are shaped by people who have spent their careers working across Life Sciences and technology — understanding the users, regulatory environments, information challenges and outcomes that matter.

Across our founders and team, Liorta brings together 110+ years of collective Life Sciences domain experience and 80+ years of technology experience.

That experience influences how we identify problems, define requirements, design features and decide what should become part of a product.

Domain-led technology — not technology looking for a problem.

How we build

Built around the problem — not around the technology.

Customer needs sit at the centre of our product decisions.

We start with the problem to be solved, the people who will use the solution, the information they depend on and the environment in which the technology must operate — and then determine how software and AI can make that work substantially more effective.

  • The user
  • The business problem
  • The information
  • The regulatory environment
  • The expected outcome

For Liorta, quality has a very practical meaning: the capabilities we commit to must work reliably, cohesively and intuitively — whether they depend on AI, conventional software or human interaction.

That standard is non-negotiable.

Founders

The people behind Liorta.

RV

Ram Venugopalan

Founder, Liorta Innovations

Ram brings more than two decades of leadership across Life Sciences technology, enterprise platforms, global delivery, product engineering, implementation, managed services and customer operations.

His experience spans building and scaling global organizations, leading complex technology transformations, shaping product strategy and working closely with Life Sciences customers to solve operational and technology challenges.

At Liorta, Ram leads the company’s platform, product and technology vision, with a strong emphasis on translating real customer problems into practical, scalable solutions.

KB

Dr. Krishna Bahadursingh

Co-Founder, Liorta Innovations

Dr. Krishna Bahadursingh is a physician and Life Sciences leader with more than three decades of experience across medicine, Patient Safety, Pharmacovigilance, Medical Affairs and pharmaceutical leadership.

Based in Japan, he brings a deep understanding of the Japanese Life Sciences environment, together with extensive experience working across global and regional organizations.

His medical background and long-standing industry experience bring an important perspective to Liorta: technology should be designed around how Life Sciences professionals actually work, the decisions they make and the regulated environments in which they operate.

Japan & global

Global perspective. Deep Japan expertise.

Liorta’s leadership includes decades of hands-on experience in Japan across pharmaceutical, medical, Patient Safety and Life Sciences functions.

With a Co-Founder based in Japan and a medical background deeply connected to the industry, Liorta brings a distinctive understanding of Japanese Life Sciences organizations, local expectations and the practical realities of connecting global technology with regional requirements.

Combined with English–Japanese capability and global Life Sciences experience, this enables Liorta to engage meaningfully with organizations operating across Japan and international markets.
